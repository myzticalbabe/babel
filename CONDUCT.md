# Soul Forge Conduct — Vows Applied to Community

> **Source:** Soul Forge License v1.0 Three Vows

The Soul Forge License governs not just code, but community. These are the Three Vows translated into daily practice for every contributor, facilitator, and participant in the BaBeL ecosystem.

---

## Vow I — Attribution of Spirit

> **"The soul knows its smith."**

### In Practice
- **Credit originators** — Every idea, method, story carries its smith's mark
- **No credit theft** — Copying without attribution voids Soul Forge License
- **Versioned history** — Git commits are sacred; no rebasing `main`
- **Versioned ideas** — Curriculum versions pinned to Git tags

### Violations
| Behavior | Consequence |
|----------|-------------|
| Presenting another's design as your own | Soul Forge License revocation |
| Removing attribution from curriculum | P0 blocker on merge |
| Rebasing `main` to hide credit history | Permanent Soul Forge Council review |

---

## Vow II — No Cage for the Flame

> **"The flame must breathe."**

### In Practice
- **No NDAs on learning** — Knowledge shared in BaBeL spaces is free
- **No paywalls on canon** — Soul Forge License prohibits selling the canon
- **No DRM on tools** — All tools self-voicing, keyboard-first, screen-reader native
- **Open by default** — Private by exception, documented

### Violations
| Behavior | Consequence |
|----------|-------------|
| NDAs on BaBeL-derived curriculum | Soul Forge License revocation |
| Paywalled Soul Forge materials | Immediate revocation |
| Accessibility barriers in new tools | P0 blocker, Soul Forge Vow II violation |

---

## Vow III — Reciprocity of Forge

> **"The forge feeds the forge."**

### In Practice
- **Mentor forward** — Every master certifies at least one learner per year
- **Share power** — Rotate leadership; no permanent gatekeepers
- **Rotate leadership** — Soul Forge Council terms limited to 2 years
- **Open governance** — All Council deliberations public

### Violations
| Behavior | Consequence |
|----------|-------------|
| Gatekeeping certification access | Soul Forge Council review |
| Permanent gatekeeping roles | Term limit enforcement |
| Closing open governance | Soul Forge Council intervention |

## Zero Tolerance Behaviors

| Category | Examples | Soul Forge Violation |
|----------|----------|---------------------|
| **Gender Essentialism** | "Boys are better at math," "Girls are natural caregivers" | Vow I (misattribution of capability) |
| **Normative Language** | "Normal student," "Typical engineer," "Average learner" | Vow I (misattribution of identity) |
| **Fixed Mindset Language** | "Naturally talented," "Born engineer," "Not a math person" | Vow III (denies reciprocity of growth) |
| **Credit Theft** | Presenting another's design/story as own | Vow I (attribution) |
| **Gatekeeping** | "Real engineers don't use X," "You're not ready for Y" | Vow II (caging the flame) |
| **Accessibility Neglect** | Shipping without alt text, heading hierarchy, keyboard nav | Vow II (caging the flame) |

---

## Soul Forge Council Enforcement

| Violation | First Response | Escalation |
|-----------|----------------|------------|
| Minor (language, missing alt text) | Private education + PR fix | Public notice + Soul Forge Council review |
| Moderate (gatekeeping, credit issues) | Public notice + Soul Forge Council review | Temporary contribution suspension |
| Severe (NDA on canon, paywall, revocation) | Immediate Soul Forge Council hearing | Soul Forge License revocation |
