# Icebreaker Library — Soul Forge Canon

> **Purpose:** Build belonging, surface identity, connect to STEM — every session starts here.

---

## Core Icebreakers (Brite Practice 1: Belonging & Relevance)

### 1. Technology Babies (Canonical)
> *"Think back to your first memory using digital technology. How old were you? What did you use? What else do you remember?"*

**Process:** Silent think (30s) → Pair share (1 min each) → 2-3 volunteers to group
**Time:** 5 min
**Variations:**
- "First memory of building something"
- "First time you fixed something broken"
- "First time you taught someone else"

**Why it works:** Connects personal history to tech identity. Everyone has a story.

---

### 2. Identity Mapping
> "Draw yourself in center. Branch to: hobbies, culture, family, languages, dreams. Then connect each to STEM."

**Process:** Solo draw (3 min) → Pair share + partner adds STEM connections (4 min) → 2 volunteers share (3 min)
**Time:** 10 min
**Materials:** Paper, markers (or digital whiteboard)

**Facilitator Prompt:** "Your abuela's recipes? Chemical engineering. Your skateboard tricks? Physics."

---

### 3. Would You Rather: STEM Jobs Edition
> Role model joins. Facilitator reads scenarios, role model shares real choice.

**Scenarios:**
- "Design a bridge spanning a canyon OR a circuit powering a village?"
- "Code an app for elders' meds OR a game teaching colors?"
- "Build a bridge for a community garden OR a sensor for air quality?"

**Role Model Responds:** Shares real choice, why, what they learned.
**Time:** 10 min (5 min scenarios + 5 min Q&A)

## Quick Icebreakers (2-3 min each)

### 4. STEM Superpower
> "If you had a STEM superpower, what would it be? What problem would you solve?"

**Format:** Round robin, 30 sec each
**Variation:** "What's your STEM origin story in 3 words?"

---

### 5. Debug Your Morning
> "Walk us through your morning routine. Where was the bug? How did you patch it?"

**Purpose:** Normalize debugging mindset. Everyone debugs daily.
**Format:** Popcorn style, 20 sec each

---

### 6. STEM Ancestor
> "Name someone in your family/community who does STEM (cooking, building, fixing, gardening, healing). What did they teach you?"

**Purpose:** Expand "who does STEM" beyond lab coats.
**Format:** Pair share, then 2-3 volunteers

---

### 7. Favorite Failure
> "Share one thing that went wrong this week. What did you try? What happened?"

**Purpose:** Normalize struggle (Brite Practice 3).
**Format:** Voluntary, facilitator models first

---

### 8. Identity Object
> "Bring an object that represents your STEM identity. 30 sec: what is it? Why?"

**Purpose:** Tangible identity connection.
**Format:** Show and tell, 45 sec each

---

## Longer Icebreakers (10-15 min)

### 9. Community Asset Mapping
> "Map your community. Where are the STEM assets? (libraries, parks, makers, elders, gardens, repair shops)"

**Process:** Small groups → large paper map → gallery walk
**Time:** 15 min
**Output:** Community STEM asset map (photo for family challenge)

---

### 10. STEM Job Charades
> Cards with STEM roles (obvious + hidden). Act out, group guesses. Debrief: "Which surprised you?"

**Roles:** Bridge inspector, flavor chemist, robot ethicist, vaccine cold-chain manager, traditional navigator, game accessibility tester, space suit seamstress
**Time:** 10 min
**Purpose:** Expand "what counts as STEM" (Brite Practice 4)

---

### 11. Identity Venn
> Three circles: "My Hobbies" ∩ "My Culture" ∩ "STEM". Fill intersections.

**Process:** Solo (3 min) → Pair share (4 min) → Group patterns (3 min)
**Time:** 10 min
**Output:** Personal identity-STEM map

---

### 12. Mentor Match Speed Round
> "If you could have coffee with any STEM person (living/historical/fictional), who? What would you ask?"

**Format:** Round robin, 45 sec each
**Purpose:** Surface role model aspirations (Brite Practice 4)

| Physical objects | Screen share photo / hold to camera |
| Charades | Act on camera / describe in chat |

---

## Icebreaker Selection Guide

| Session Goal | Recommended |
|--------------|-------------|
| First session ever | Technology Babies + Identity Mapping |
| New group, short time | Technology Babies (5 min) |
| Deep identity work | Identity Mapping (10 min) |
| Role model session | Would You Rather: STEM Jobs |
| Struggle normalization | Favorite Failure / Debug Your Morning |
| Community connection | Community Asset Mapping |
| Virtual session | Technology Babies + Chat waterfall |

---

## Facilitator Notes

- **Model first:** Always share your own answer first
- **Timebox:** Use visible timer
- **Voluntary sharing:** "Pass" is always OK
- **Multilingual:** Key prompts in EN + ES minimum
- **Accessibility:** Verbal + visual instructions, alt text for images
