# Facilitation Skills — Practical Techniques

> **Soul Forge Principle:** "The forge feeds the forge." Every facilitator teaches facilitation.

---

## Core Facilitation Moves

### 1. The Three-Before-Me Protocol
**Purpose:** Normalize struggle, build peer capacity, reduce facilitator bottleneck.

```
Learner stuck → Self (30 sec) → Peer (1 min) → Resource (1 min) → Facilitator
```

**Facilitator Script:**
> "What have you tried? What did your partner suggest? What does the diagram show?"

**When to break protocol:** Safety issue, time-critical, learner in distress.

---

### 2. Struggle Journal Facilitation
**Purpose:** Make struggle visible, normalize it, track growth.

**Journal Template (every session):**
```
Date: ______ Activity: ______

STUCK POINT: _______________________________________
STRATEGY TRIED: ____________________________________
RESULT: ___________________________________________
NEXT STRATEGY: ____________________________________

IDENTITY CHECK: How does this connect to who I am / who I want to be?
________________________________________________________
```

**Facilitator Moves:**
- **Model it:** Share your own journal entry weekly
- **Celebrate failures:** "That's data! What did it tell you?"
- **Prompt reflection:** "What would you tell someone else who gets stuck here?"

---

### 3. Gallery Walk Protocol
**Purpose:** Peer feedback, public iteration, authentic audience.

**Structure (15 min):**
1. **Display (2 min):** Half groups post work; half grab feedback forms
2. **Visit (6 min):** Visitors leave "I like..." + "What if..." on each project
3. **Switch (6 min):** Roles reverse
4. **Synthesize (1 min):** Hosts read feedback, note one change

**Feedback Form:**
```
Project: ___________
I like: _________________________________________
What if: _______________________________________
Signed: ___________ (visitor)
```

---

### 4. Identity Mapping Icebreaker
**Purpose:** Connect STEM to self, build belonging (Brite Practice 1).

**Process (10 min):**
1. **Solo (3 min):** Draw yourself in center. Branch to: hobbies, culture, family, languages, dreams
2. **Pair (4 min):** Share map. Partner adds: "That connects to STEM because..."
3. **Group (3 min):** Volunteers share one surprise connection

**Facilitator Prompt:** "Your abuela's recipes? That's chemical engineering. Your skateboard tricks? That's physics."

---

### 5. Technology Babies (Brite Practice 1)
**Purpose:** Connect personal history to technology identity.

**Prompt:** *"Think back to your first memory using digital technology. How old were you? What did you use? What else do you remember?"*

**Process:**
- Silent think (30 sec)
- Elbow partner share (1 min each)
- 2-3 volunteers to group

**Variations:**
- "First memory of building something"
- "First time you fixed something broken"
- "First time you taught someone else"

---

### 6. Would You Rather: STEM Jobs Edition
**Purpose:** Expose diverse STEM careers, challenge stereotypes (Brite Practice 4).

**Format:** Role model joins live or via video. Facilitator reads scenarios:
> "Would you rather design a bridge that spans a canyon OR a circuit that powers a village?"

**Role Model Responds:** Shares real choice they made, why, what they learned.

## Session Flow Templates

### Standard 45-Minute Session
| Time | Segment | Facilitator Role |
|------|---------|------------------|
| 0-5 | Icebreaker (Identity Mapping / Tech Babies) | Model vulnerability first |
| 5-10 | Role Model Story (5 min) | Introduce, step back |
| 10-25 | Investigation (build/test/debug) | "Three before me", document strategies |
| 25-37 | Design for Community | Pair different strengths, co-design |
| 37-45 | Gallery Walk + Reflection | Facilitate feedback, close with identity check |

### 90-Minute Deep Dive
| Time | Segment |
|------|---------|
| 0-10 | Icebreaker + Identity Mapping |
| 10-20 | Role Model Story + Q&A |
| 20-50 | Investigation (extended build + struggle journal) |
| 50-65 | Design for Community (co-design) |
| 65-80 | Gallery Walk + Iteration |
| 80-90 | Identity Reflection + Family Challenge |

## Facilitator Self-Check (Before Every Session)

- [ ] **I've tested the activity myself** (no surprises)
- [ ] **Role model story ready** (printed/digital)
- [ ] **Materials stations set** (pre-cut, pre-torn, labeled)
- [ ] **Struggle Journal templates** (one per learner)
- [ ] **Visual sequence cards** (max 3 steps each)
- [ ] **Bilingual glossary** (EN/ES minimum)
- [ ] **Accessibility check** (alt text, font size, contrast, keyboard nav)
- [ ] **My own Struggle Journal entry** ready to share

## When Things Go Sideways

| Situation | Facilitator Move |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Learner gives up** | "What's the smallest next step?" + sit beside |
| **Group dominates** | "Let's hear from someone who hasn't spoken" + redirect |
| **Safety issue** | Stop immediately, address calmly, document |
| **Tech fails** | "Analog version:" sketch on paper, act out circuit |
| **Learner in distress** | Pause, private check-in, "What do you need right now?" |
