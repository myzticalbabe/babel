# Family Engagement Pack — Templates & Protocols

> **Soul Forge Principle:** "The forge feeds the forge." Families are the first mentors.

---

## Core Principle

Every session sends home a **Family Challenge** — not homework, but a conversation starter using household materials. Multilingual (EN/ES minimum), offline-first, ≤$0 cost.

## Family Challenges by Topic (Copy-Paste Ready)

### Circuits
> **Home Challenge:** Find one thing in your home that uses a circuit. Draw it together. Talk about: What problem does it solve for your family? (Examples: nightlight, phone charger, microwave, bike light)

### Bridges
> **Home Challenge:** Find one bridge in your community (over a stream, highway, park path). Draw it together. Who uses it? What would happen without it?

### Coding
> **Home Challenge:** Think of one thing in daily life that could be easier with a simple program. Draw the screens together. (Examples: medication reminder for abuela, color game for little cousin, recipe share for family)

### Ecosystems
> **Home Challenge:** Observe one small ecosystem near home (puddle, garden corner, park tree). Draw and label what you see: producers, consumers, decomposers.

### Rockets
> **Home Challenge:** Design a paper rocket together. Test different fin shapes. Which flies farthest? Measure with footsteps.

## Family Engagement Protocols

### Timing
- **Send same day** as session (memory fresh)
- **Weekend window** for completion (families busy weekdays)
- **Follow-up Monday** with Community Gallery highlights

### Accessibility
- **EN + ES minimum** (every email)
- **Printable version** attached (PDF, ≤2 pages)
- **Offline option:** Paper copy sent home if no email
- **Low-tech option:** Challenge uses only household items
- **Audio option:** WhatsApp voice note with challenge

### Cultural Responsiveness
- **Names:** Use family's preferred names
- **Examples:** Include diverse family structures (abuela, two moms, foster, chosen family)
- **Materials:** Assume $0 budget (foil, battery, paper, tape, jars, sticks)
- **Space:** Apartment-friendly (no yard required)

## Community Gallery (Closing the Loop)

### Physical Gallery
- **Location:** School/library/community center hallway
- **Duration:** 2 weeks per unit
- **Opening:** Family night with learner presentations

### Digital Gallery
- **Platform:** Private GitHub Discussions / Padlet / Google Sites
- **Structure:** One post per learner (photo + 2-sentence caption)
- **Interaction:** Comments enabled ("I like..." + "What if...")
- **Moderation:** Facilitator approves all posts

### Family Night Script (30 min)
1. **Welcome** (5 min) — Facilitator shares unit story
2. **Gallery Walk** (15 min) — Families visit, leave sticky-note feedback
3. **Learner Spotlight** (5 min) — 2-3 learners share "What I learned about myself"
4. **Next Unit Preview** (5 min) — Tease next Family Challenge
