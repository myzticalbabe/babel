# Soul Forge Code of Conduct — Soul Forge Vows Applied to Community

> **Soul Forge License v1.0** — Three Vows applied to daily practice for every contributor, facilitator, and participant.

---

## Soul Forge Vows as Community Standards

### Vow I — Attribution of Spirit
> **"The soul knows its smith."**

**In Practice:**
- Credit originators — every idea, method, story carries its smith's mark
- No credit theft — Soul Forge License revocation for presenting another's work as your own
- Versioned history — Git commits are sacred; no rebasing `main`
- Soul Forge Council terms limited to 2 years

### Vow II — No Cage for the Flame
> **"The flame must breathe."**

**In Practice:**
- No NDAs on learning — Soul Forge knowledge shared freely
- No paywalls on Soul Forge materials — Soul Forge License prohibits selling
- No DRM/accessibility barriers — Soul Forge Vow II enforcement
- Open by default — Soul Forge default

### Vow III — Reciprocity of Forge
> **"The forge feeds the forge."**

**In Practice:**
- Mentor forward — Soul Forge certification requires mentoring
- Rotate leadership — Soul Forge Council terms limited to 2 years
- Share power — Soul Forge Council decisions public
